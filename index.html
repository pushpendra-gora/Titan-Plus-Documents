Privacy Policy

Zero-Knowledge Architecture • 100% Private
Titan Plus Ecosystem • Privacy Guarantee

1. 🔒 100% On-Device & Offline Storage

All your documents, photographs, PDFs, and binary files are stored exclusively inside your device's sandboxed local IndexedDB storage. No files, metadata, or document contents are ever transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party endpoints.

2. 🚫 Zero Telemetry, Analytics & Advertising

Titan Documents contains zero tracking SDKs, telemetry libraries, advertising identifiers, or analytics scripts. Your document interactions, search queries, file views, and application usage patterns remain strictly private and confidential to you.

3. 🔐 App Lock & Local Security Architecture

Your 4-digit security PIN is hashed and retained strictly in local device storage. The PIN is never synced over any network or accessible to outside entities, preventing unauthorized access even if your phone is unlocked.

4. 📦 Complete User Ownership & Data Portability

You retain 100% ownership and control over your files. You can export a lossless ZIP backup at any time via "Backup all files into zip", share the unified archive via any app, or permanently delete documents with a single tap.

5. 📲 Secure System File Provider

External applications can access a document only when you explicitly select it through the Android file picker or share sheet. File permissions are temporary, scoped to the active session, and automatically revoked thereafter.
